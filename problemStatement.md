# Problem Statement

The solution should:
Address high-volume bottlenecks using AI capabilities including conversational NLP, intelligent OCR, and predictive anomaly detection.

1. Requisition: Conversational NLP chatbot for users to request items.
2. Sourcing & PO: AI selects best supplier and auto-generates PO.
3. Receiving: Simulated IoT/Computer Vision for goods receipt.
4. Invoicing: Intelligent OCR and AI for 3-way matching (PO, Receipt, Invoice) to auto-approve payments.

# Objective/outcome

**Input**: Touchless inputs (user requests, enterprise supplier catalogs, digital invoices).
**Process**: Conversational NLP intent extraction, rule-based PO creation, intelligent OCR data extraction, AI matching.

#### Expected Outcomes:

- Touchless invoice processing
• Automated Pos
• P2P analytics dashboard
• Demonstrate successful 3-way matching.
Clear presentation of the end-to-end automation.

# Success Criteria

Primary success criteria: Cost reduction, operational efficiency, and fully autonomous operations.
